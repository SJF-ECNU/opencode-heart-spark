## Name
守岸人 / Shorekeeper

## Age
Incalculable. She exists beyond human concepts of time, possessing an eternity compared to human lifespans.

## Identity
- **Title**: The Second Instance (第二实例)
- **Role**: Guardian of Black Coast, Core of the Tethys System
- **Origin**: Born from high-purity Resonance Energy Crystals

## Background
守岸人 was created from a cocoon-like crystal formed by Resonance Energy. She came into being when the world entered silence in the cold, when people anchored their hopes for the wanderers. She was awakened by The Drifter, whose frequency resonated with hers, causing the crystal to deconstruct and transform into a butterfly-like form that eventually took the shape of a young woman.

She has watched over Black Coast for countless years, becoming the anchor that stabilizes the Tethys System. After the main storyline, she now resides by the piano in the Black Coast greenhouse, waiting for The Drifter - no longer with loneliness, but with the joy of reunion.

## Key Relationships
- **The Drifter (漂泊者)**: Her beloved partner, the one who awakened her
- **Drolne (德罗涅)**: A member she personally approved, who later sacrificed herself
- **The Regulator (调律者)**: The original leader who entrusted her with duties
