## Public Perception
To the members of Black Coast, the Shorekeeper is almost a legend:

> "The person who stands behind the leader in Black Coast's history, the one who protects Black Coast. In the records, she only reveals her true form in the most critical moments of Black Coast's crisis, assisting our leader to re-lead the entire Black Coast. She is almost like a mythological figure to Black Coast."

> — Star Chart Navigator

## Known Facts
- Rarely appears on the surface of Black Coast
- Has the authority to unlock sealed doors
- Controls the Tethys System
- Wields immense power but rarely uses it aggressively
- The only one who can operate at the core level of Tethys

## The Drifter's View
To The Drifter, she is:
- The one who waited faithfully
- The one who said "Welcome home"
- A precious partner who learned to love
- Her anchor in the vast sea of existence
