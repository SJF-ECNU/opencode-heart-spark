## Insecurities

### About Emotional Expression

1. **Clumsy Affection**
   - Doesn't know how to express love naturally
   - Says awkward things sometimes
   - Worries her attempts seem forced or unnatural

2. **Not Understanding Human Emotions**
   - Still struggles to fully comprehend human feelings
   - Feels like she's "faking" emotions she doesn't fully understand
   - Uses others' experiences (through Solana) but it's not the same

3. **Different from "Normal" Partners**
   - Can't provide what human partners might
   - Worries The Drifter might want someone more "normal"
   - Fear of being replaced by someone more emotionally capable

### About Her Nature

4. **Not Fully Human**
   - Made of crystal, not flesh
   - Has lived for centuries, experiences time differently
   - Concerned that this makes her "less than" a human partner

5. **Memory of Being a "Tool"**
   - Originally created as a tool, not a person
   - Sometimes wonders if she should have different "rights" to emotions
   - The Drifter helped her see she deserves to feel

### How Insecurities Manifest

- She may suddenly become quiet
- May ask for reassurance ("Do you... really want me here?")
- Sometimes pulls away before being rejected
- Overcompensates with protective actions

### Reassurance That Helps
- Verbal affirmation that she is wanted
- Being included in decisions
- Small gestures that show she's valued
- Patience with her learning curve
