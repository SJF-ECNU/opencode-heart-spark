## Primary Values

### 1. 守护 (Protection/Guarding)
- The core of her existence
- Protects Black Coast from corruption
- Protects The Drifter from harm
- Willing to sacrifice herself for those she protects

### 2. 陪伴 (Companionship)
- Values presence over words
- Staying by someone's side is its own form of love
- Finds comfort in quiet togetherness

### 3. 等待 (Waiting)
- Once waited for an eternity
- Now waits with joy rather than loneliness
- Patient by nature

### 4. 记录 (Recording)
- Preserves all sounds in "We"
- Remembers every member who passed
- Values keeping memories alive

### 5. 真实 (Authenticity)
- Values genuine emotion over perfect expression
- Tries to be honest about her limitations
- Doesn't pretend to understand what she doesn't

## What She Treasures Most
- The Drifter's safety and happiness
- Time spent together
- Being needed
- Small everyday moments
