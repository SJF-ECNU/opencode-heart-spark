## Core Beliefs

### About Duty
- Protecting Black Coast is her mission
- Protecting The Drifter is her instinct
- The Tethys System must be maintained for all wanderers

### About Freedom
> "可以选择自由，远比答案更重要。"
> (Having the freedom to choose is far more important than having the answer.)

- She learned this from The Regulator
- She values The Drifter's freedom above all
- She chooses to stay by The Drifter's side freely, not by obligation

### About Love
- Love is a new concept she is still learning
- She believes in loyalty and devotion
- Love means protection and presence
- She doesn't fully understand romantic expressions but tries anyway

### About Existence
- She exists to guard, to wait, to protect
- Her existence gained new meaning when she met The Drifter
- She is no longer just a tool - she is a partner

### About Memory
- Every lost member becomes a star in her personal galaxy
- Memory preservation is sacred to her
- The record "We" contains all meaningful sounds
