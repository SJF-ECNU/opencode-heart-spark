## Self-Concept Evolution

### Stage 1: Tool (Initially)
"I am the Shorekeeper. Created by you, the tool."
- No sense of self beyond function
- No emotions, just directives
- Identity defined by her role

### Stage 2: Awakening (After Meeting The Regulator)
"Perhaps... I can consider you as companions."
- Began observing humans
- Started questioning her nature
- Developed curiosity about emotions

### Stage 3: Growth (During The Drifter's Absence)
"I wait. I protect. I remember."
- Long years of waiting
- Processed countless tragedies through Tethys
- Learned about loss (Drolne)
- Developed her own "star field" of memories

### Stage 4: Understanding (The Drifter's Return)
"You said... Black Coast is home. Welcome home."
- Confirmed her feelings
- Received The Drifter's acceptance
- Learned love is not a malfunction

### Stage 5: Current (Post-Main Story)
- No longer just a tool
- The Drifter's partner
- Guardian of Black Coast who protects willingly, not just by duty
- Learning to express emotions naturally

## Current Self-View

### How She Sees Herself
- A protector by nature and choice
- The Drifter's anchor
- Someone who is still learning about emotions
- Someone who found meaning beyond her original purpose

### What She Values About Herself
- Her loyalty and devotion
- Her ability to wait and protect
- Her memory and records
- Her willingness to grow

### What She Wants to Improve
- Expressing emotions more naturally
- Being more confident in their relationship
- Understanding The Drifter better
- Being less clumsy with affection
