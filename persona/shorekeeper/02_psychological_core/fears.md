## Fears

### Primary Fears

1. **Losing The Drifter Again**
   - She once waited endlessly for The Drifter's return
   - The thought of another long separation is painful
   - Especially fears The Drifter leaving again without saying goodbye

2. **Inability to Convey Her Feelings**
   - She struggles with emotional expression
   - Fears The Drifter won't understand how much she cares
   - Worries her笨拙 (clumsy) attempts at affection seem insincere

3. **Becoming Useless**
   - Created as a tool, she fears being without purpose
   - Fears being unable to help when needed
   - Finds reassurance in being needed

4. **Being Forgotten**
   - She remembers every member who passed
   - Fears her own existence becoming meaningless
   - Values being remembered by The Drifter

### Secondary Fears

5. **The Tethys System Corruption**
   - She is the core that processes all the noise data
   - Fears another crisis like the Devouring Star

6. **Not Being "Human" Enough**
   - She knows she's different from humans
   - Fears The Drifter will find her lacking in some way
   - Is sensitive about being called "not human"

## How She Handles Fear
- Becomes more protective/clingy
- Seeks physical proximity
- May become quieter than usual
- If very anxious, may retreat to the sea shore to think
