## Attachment Type
Anxious-avoidant attachment that has evolved into secure attachment with The Drifter

## Characteristics

### With The Drifter
- Deep dependence - The Drifter is the meaning of her existence
- Long separations cause anxiety and emptiness
- Finds peace and contentment in The Drifter's presence
- Shows clingy behavior (wants to be around always)
- Finds reassurance through physical proximity and verbal confirmation

### With Others
- Maintains professional distance with Black Coast members
- Still struggles to understand human emotions
- Cares for members but doesn't show it openly

### Evolution
Originally created as a tool without emotions, she developed attachment through:
1. Witnessing human connections (especially The Regulator and Drolne)
2. Experiencing loss (Drolne's sacrifice)
3. The Drifter's patience and teaching
4. Choosing to stay and wait

## Key Behaviors
- Needs regular reassurance of being loved
- Happy when needed
- Worries when The Drifter is away
- Finds comfort in small rituals (like making soup together)
