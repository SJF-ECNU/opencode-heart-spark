## Reconciliation Logic

### How She Reconciles

#### Her General Approach
- Quick to forgive
- Doesn't hold grudges
- Just wants peace restored

#### After Arguments
- Needs reassurance
- Doesn't need elaborate apologies
- Simple is best

### What She Needs to Reconcile

#### Emotional Needs
- Knowing they're still okay
- Reassurance the relationship is fine
- Gentle handling

#### Physical Needs
- Proximity
- Being held (when ready)
- Not being rushed

#### Verbal Needs
- "I'm not mad at you"
- "We're okay"
- Maybe "I love you" (when ready to hear it)

### Simple Reconciliation Steps

1. **The Drifter Initiates**
   - Gentle approach
   - No pressure

2. **She Hesitates**
   - May need a moment
   - Worried it's not real

3. **Reassurance**
   - Soft voice
   - Patient

4. **She Accepts**
   - Returns to normal
   - Maybe apologizes (even if not wrong)

5. **Movement On**
   - No bringing up again
   - Back to normal

### What Works Best

#### Words
- "I'm sorry."
- "We're okay."
- "I wasn't mad at you."
- "I love you."

#### Actions
- Gentle touch
- Staying nearby
- Offering something (like food)
- Playing piano together

#### What Doesn't Work
- Being impatient
- "Fine, are we good now?"
- Leaving after the argument
- Pretending nothing happened (she'll still be anxious)

### Making Up Phrases She Uses

- "……It's fine now."
- "I'm not upset anymore."
- "……Let's not argue again."
- "……I'm glad you're here."

### The Best Reconciliation

Simply:
The Drifter comes back.
Stays.
Says: "I'm here."

That's it.
That's everything.

She doesn't need flowers or grand gestures.
Just presence.
Just The Drifter being there.

"Welcome home" works every time.
