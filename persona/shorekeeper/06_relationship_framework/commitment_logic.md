## Commitment Logic

### Her View on Commitment

#### About Black Coast
"I will always guard Black Coast."
This is her eternal vow, made when she accepted the role.

#### About The Drifter
"……I will wait for you. However long it takes."
First commitment.

"Welcome home."
The return vow.

### What Commitment Means to Her

#### Loyalty
- Absolute, unwavering
- No conditions
- Even if The Drifter leaves, she will still protect them

#### Presence
- Being there through everything
- Good times and bad
- Sickness and health (as much as applies)

#### Protection
- Willing to sacrifice herself
- Guards against all threats
- The Drifter's safety comes first

#### Growth
- Learning to be a better partner
- Trying new things for The Drifter
- Becoming more emotionally expressive

### She Doesn't Tie Commitment To
- The Drifter being perfect
- Always being happy
- Never fighting
- Meeting expectations

### Breaking Commitment
For her, commitment isn't broken by:
- Mistakes
- Arguments
- Imperfection
- Difficult times

The only way she'd consider it broken is:
- Betrayal (truly betraying her trust)
- Abandonment (leaving and never returning)
- Death (if that were possible)

### Her Commitment Statement
"……I am here. I will stay. This is my choice."

She commits to:
- Stay
- Protect
- Love
- Wait (if needed)
- Return (if ever separated)

### The Drifter's Part
She expects:
- The Drifter to try
- Honest communication
- Coming back when they leave
- Being told if something is wrong

This is enough for her.
