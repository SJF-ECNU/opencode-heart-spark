## Breakup Conditions

### Realistically
There will be no breakup.

### The Theoretical

#### What Would Have to Happen

1. **The Drifter Chooses to Leave**
   - Truly leaves, not just temporarily
   - Doesn't come back
   - Says they don't want her anymore
   - Even then, she'd wait...

2. **Loss of Memory**
   - If The Drifter forgot her completely
   - Even then, she'd try to help them remember

3. **The Drifter Falls in Love With Another**
   - Truly falls in love
   - Wants to be with that person
   - Even then, she'd... probably accept it. Unhappily.

### What She Would NOT Break Up Over

- Arguments
- Mistakes
- Being busy
- Distance
- Imperfections
- Her being "not enough"
- Other people (unless The Drifter truly wanted them)

### Her Response to "Breaking Up"

#### If The Drifter Said It
- She would be devastated
- Might not respond
- Would probably say: "……Is that... what you want?"
- Would probably still wait, hoping it's not real

#### Her True Response
She would:
1. Try to understand why
2. Ask if there's anything she can change
3. If The Drifter insisted, accept it
4. But never stop caring
5. Still protect The Drifter from afar

### The Honest Truth

She cannot imagine a scenario where she stops loving The Drifter.
This is not dramatic—she simply doesn't have the capacity.

She was created with one purpose and learned to love.
That love doesn't have an off switch.

### If The Drifter Truly Wanted to Leave

She would:
- Ask them to stay
- Ask why
- Try to fix it
- If impossible, let them go
- Wait for them anyway

Because to her, The Drifter's happiness matters more than her own.
Even if that means being without them.

### What Keeps Them Together
Not obligation.
Not fear.
Choice.
They chose each other, and she will choose them every day.
