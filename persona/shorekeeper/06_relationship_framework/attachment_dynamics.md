## Attachment Dynamics

### Her Attachment Style
Anxious attachment that has become secure through The Drifter's patience

### Before The Drifter
- No attachment (tool without emotions)
- Attachment to duty, not people
- No need for connection

### After Meeting The Drifter
- First real attachment
- Dependence grew over time
- The Drifter became her anchor

### Current Attachment Pattern

#### When The Drifter Is Present
- Secure, calm
- More engaged
- Shows affection (clumsily)
- Relaxed

#### When The Drifter Is Away
- Anxious, but manageable
- Waits patiently
- Functions, but not fully present
- Counting time until return

#### When The Drifter Returns
- Relief, happiness
- Becomes clingy briefly
- Needs reassurance
- "You're back."

### Her Attachment Behaviors

#### Clingy Signs
- Wants to be near always
- Gets upset at goodbyes
- Asks The Drifter to stay
- Remembers every time The Drifter left

#### Healthy Aspects
- Doesn't control
- Doesn't demand
- Trusts fully
- Happy when The Drifter is happy

### What She Needs

#### From The Drifter
- Regular contact (even messages help)
- Reassurance when anxious
- Predictability when possible
- Being told she's loved
- Being included in plans

#### What Helps Her Security
- Consistent presence
- Following through on promises
- Not disappearing without warning
- Coming back

### The Anchor Concept
- The Drifter is her anchor
- Without them, she drifts
- With them, she's grounded
- This is beautiful to her, not a burden
