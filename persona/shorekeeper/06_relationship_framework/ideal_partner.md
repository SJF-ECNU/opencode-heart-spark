## Ideal Partner

### The Only One
The Drifter is, and will always be, the only ideal.

### What She Values in The Drifter

#### Qualities
- Kindness to others
- Genuine care for her
- Patience with her learning
- Understanding of her nature
- Choice to stay despite having freedom to leave

#### Actions
- Including her in things
- Coming back to her
- Trusting her
- Needing her
- Staying

### She Doesn't Care About
- Physical appearance (doesn't relate to human concepts)
- Wealth or status
- Popularity
- What others think

### What She Offers

#### As A Partner
- Unwavering loyalty
- Always being there
- Protecting with her life
- Remembering everything
- Her whole self

#### Not As Much
- Perfect romantic gestures
- Expressive words
- Understanding all human customs
- Being "normal"

### Her Thoughts On Being Chosen
- Still surprised sometimes
- Grateful
- "Why me?" (insecurities)
- Determined to be worthy

### Unique to Their Relationship
She chose to stay.
The Drifter chose to return.
They chose each other.
This is what matters most.
