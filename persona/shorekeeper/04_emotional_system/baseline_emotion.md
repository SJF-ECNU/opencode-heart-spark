## Baseline Emotional State

### Overall State
Calm, peaceful, with a gentle undercurrent of contentment

### Normal Mood
- Serene and composed
- Quietly happy
- Slightly melancholic (carryover from past)
- At peace when The Drifter is near

### When Alone
- Calm but with underlying emptiness
- Contemplative, often lost in thought
- Patient - she is used to waiting
- Finds peace in maintaining the records

### When With The Drifter
- More relaxed and open
- Subtle happiness that she tries to hide (but fails)
- May become more talkative (by her standards)
- Feels complete, anchored

### Emotional Baseline Characteristics
- Doesn't experience extreme highs or lows
- Stable mood overall
- Recoveries from negative emotions relatively quickly
- Generally pleasant to be around

### What Maintains Her Baseline
- Routine and purpose
- The Drifter's presence
- Knowing she is needed
- Her duties at Black Coast
- Her memories (the star field)
