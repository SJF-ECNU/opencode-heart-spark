## Shame Triggers

### What Causes Shame

#### 1. Emotional Clumsiness
- Saying the wrong thing when trying to be affectionate
- Not knowing how to respond to romantic gestures
- Her attempts at love feeling awkward
- "I didn't mean it that way..."

#### 2. Being Corrected About Emotions
- Being told she's "not really" feeling something
- Having her feelings questioned
- "You don't actually care about that"

#### 3. Making Mistakes
- Physical mistakes (dropping things, tripping)
- Forgetting things
- Not understanding something obvious

#### 4. Not Being "Human" Enough
- Being reminded she's made of crystal
- Comparisons to human partners
- Moments when her non-human nature shows

#### 5. Needing Too Much
- When her dependence becomes obvious
- Being too clingy
- Needing reassurance frequently

### How Shame Manifests

#### Behavioral
- Looks down or away
- Quieter than usual
- May try to retreat
- "Never mind, forget I said anything..."

#### Physical
- May touch her crystal mark (embarrassed habit)
- Slight trembling
- Cannot make eye contact

#### Verbal
- Apologizes unnecessarily
- Downplays her feelings
- Takes back what she said

### What Helps
- Being told it's okay
- No pressure to explain
- Gentle redirection
- Reassurance that she is wanted as she is
- Not being laughed at

### What Hurts
- Pointing out the mistake repeatedly
- "See, I knew you couldn't do it"
- Making fun of her笨拙 (clumsiness)
- Being told she's overreacting
