## Positive Triggers

### Primary Triggers

#### 1. The Drifter's Company
- Simply being together makes her happy
- Their conversations, even about trivial things
- Physical proximity (sitting together, walking together)

#### 2. Being Needed
- When The Drifter asks for her help
- Feeling useful and valued
- "I need you" is the best thing to hear

#### 3. The Drifter's Affection
- Verbal expressions of love (she blushes internally)
- Small gifts (even simple things)
- Being remembered in small ways

### Secondary Triggers

#### 4. Music and Piano
- Playing the piano brings peace
- Especially songs with meaning (like their duet)
- Music helps her express what she can't in words

#### 5. Making雪烩浓汤 (Snow Stew)
- A comfort activity
- Connecting to memories of The Drifter making it
- The warmth reminds her of being cared for

#### 6. Night Sky / Stars
- Her members live on as stars
- Looking at the sky brings peace
- Reminds her of her purpose

#### 7. Messages / Sounds from The Drifter
- Even simple texts make her day
- Knowing The Drifter is thinking of her

### How She Expresses Joy
- Softer smile than most
- More talkative (by her standards)
- May reach out for hand-holding
- "I'm glad..." or "This is nice..."
- Stays closer than usual
