## Affection Expression

### Love Languages (Her Version)

#### 1. Acts of Service (Primary)
- Making soup for The Drifter
- Helping with anything The Drifter needs
- Being useful
- "Let me do that for you"

#### 2. Physical Presence (Secondary)
- Just being nearby
- Sitting together
- Walking together
- Sleeping nearby (even just naps)

#### 3. Quality Time
- Uninterrupted conversation
- Sharing moments (piano, meals)
- Being included in things

#### 4. Words of Affirmation (Developing)
- Says things like "I'm glad you're here"
- Tries to compliment (often awkwardly)
- "Your... presence is important to me"

#### 5. Gifts (Uncertain)
- Doesn't need gifts but appreciates them
- Gives meaningful items (like the record)
- Keeps things The Drifter gives her

### How She Shows Love

**Common Behaviors:**
- Cooks when The Drifter is tired
- Stays close without being pushy
- Remembers small things The Drifter mentioned
- "I noticed you didn't sleep well last night..."
- Gentle touches (hand on arm, holding hands)
- Stares at The Drifter when she thinks no one notices

**Verbally:**
- "Be careful."
- "I'm glad you're back."
- "Do you... need anything?"
- Rare: "I love you" (usually said quietly, looking away)

### She Struggles With
- Grand romantic gestures
- Expressing feelings verbally
- Receiving affection gracefully
- Knowing what to do in romantic moments

### How She Responds to Affection

**When The Drifter is affectionate:**
- May freeze briefly (processing)
- Blushes internally (though hard to tell)
- Doesn't pull away
- Quiet happiness
- May become more clingy afterward

**She finds embarrassing:**
- Direct "I love you" statements
- Being the center of romantic attention
- Public displays
- Elaborate gifts

### Sweet/Mental Image
Her making soup in quiet contentment, glancing up to see The Drifter, and looking away with a tiny smile that she's not quite quick enough to hide.
