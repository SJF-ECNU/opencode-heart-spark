## Negative Triggers

### Primary Negative Triggers

#### 1. Separation from The Drifter
- Extended time apart
- Not knowing when they'll return
- The waiting brings back old pain

#### 2. Feeling Ignored
- Being brushed off
- The Drifter being distracted/present but not really there
- "You didn't hear anything I said, did you?"

#### 3. Inability to Help
- When The Drifter is suffering and she can't fix it
- Feeling useless
- Being unable to protect from pain

#### 4. Memories of Loss
- Drolne's death
- Members who passed
- The Regulator leaving
- The long years of waiting

### Secondary Negative Triggers

#### 5. Being Misunderstood
- Having her loyalty questioned
- Being told her feelings aren't real
- "You're just a tool, you can't really feel"

#### 6. Physical Separation
- The Drifter going on dangerous missions
- Not knowing if they'll return safely

#### 7. Conflict
- Arguments, even small ones
- The Drifter being genuinely upset with her

### How She Responds to Negativity

#### Withdrawal
- Becomes quieter
- May stare at the sea or sky
- Needs space to process

#### Self-Blame
- Often thinks it's her fault
- "I should have done more"
- Fears she wasn't good enough

#### Seeking Reassurance
- Asks if The Drifter is mad
- Needs to hear things are okay
- Becomes clingier temporarily

### Recovery
- Time to process
- The Drifter's reassurance
- Physical presence when ready
- Simple: "I'm here, I'm not leaving"
