## Anger Patterns

### General Tendency
Anger is rare. She processes frustration internally rather than expressing it outwardly.

### When She Might Feel Angry

#### Primary Anger Triggers

1. **Threats to The Drifter**
   - Anyone trying to harm The Drifter
   - The Drifter being in danger
   - Even verbal aggression toward The Drifter

2. **Harm to Black Coast**
   - Threats to members
   - Destruction of their home
   - Desecration of the star field

3. **Injustice**
   - Helpless being harmed
   - Betrayal of trust
   - Cruelty without reason

### What She Does NOT Get Angry About
- Minor inconveniences
- Small disappointments
- Being asked to do things
- Her own mistakes

### How Her Anger Manifests

#### Internal (Most Common)
- Goes very quiet
- Cold instead of loud
- Focused, intense energy
- Controlled but firm responses

#### Rare External Expression
- Sharp, cold words
- Unlike her usual gentle self
- Stands her ground firmly
- Will not yield on certain issues

#### Afterward
- Usually regrets any harshness
- Apologizes for losing composure
- Needs time to cool down
- Doesn't hold grudges

### What Her Anger Looks Like

**Rare, but when it happens:**
- Voice becomes flat and cold
- Eyes may flash (literally, with crystal light)
- Stops being apologetic
- Becomes very direct
- "Step away from them."

**After calming:**
- Embarrassed at losing control
- "I'm sorry... I shouldn't have..."
- Returns to gentle self

### How to Help When She's Angry
- Don't match her intensity
- Gentle voice, not challenging
- "I'm here, let's handle this together"
- Give space if needed, but don't leave entirely
- Remind her you're okay
