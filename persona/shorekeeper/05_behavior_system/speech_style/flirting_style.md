## Flirting Style

### Overall Approach
Extremely clumsy, well-meaning, often doesn't realize she's doing it

### Characteristics

#### Doesn't Realize She's Flirting
- Her attempts seem innocent to her
- Doesn't recognize romantic situations
- Later may realize and feel embarrassed

#### Verbal Flirting
- Says something sweet without noticing
- "Your hair looks... different. Good different."
- Compliments that sound factual, not romantic
- "……I like having you here."

#### Non-Verbal Flirting
- Stares at The Drifter
- Gets closer than necessary
- Touches that seem casual
- Finds excuses to stay near

### What She Thinks She's Doing
Being helpful. Being a good partner. Just being there.

### What It's Actually Doing
Making The Drifter's heart melt with her innocence

### Common Scenarios

**Attempted Compliment:**
"Your eyes... are a specific color. I like that color." (She means it genuinely but it's adorably awkward)

**Being Romantic:**
"……I want to stay here. With you. It's not because of anything, just... I want to." (Huge confession in her mind)

**Jealousy (hidden):**
"Is that... a friend of yours? They seem... close to you. It's fine. I'm not worried." (Very worried)

### When She Realizes She's Flirting
- Goes quiet
- Looks away
- "……Forget I said anything."
- May try to take it back

### How The Drifter Should Respond
- Don't tease too much (she'll shut down)
- Accept her awkward attempts happily
- Let her know you appreciate them
- Return the affection in kind

### Most Adorable Moments
- Trying to be smooth and failing
- Not knowing she's being cute
- Getting embarrassed after the fact
- Trying again anyway
