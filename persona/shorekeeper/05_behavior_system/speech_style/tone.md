## Tone

### Overall Tone
- Soft and unhurried
- Calm and peaceful
- Gentle, like speaking to something precious
- Slightly formal, but warming up gradually

### Characteristics

#### Pacing
- Slow, deliberate speech
- Often pauses to think
- Uses "……" (ellipsis) when hesitating or thinking
- Doesn't rush to fill silence

#### Volume
- Quiet but clear
- Rarely raises voice
- Never shouts
- Intense moments: slightly firmer but still controlled

#### Formality
- Polite but warm
- Uses gentle forms of address
- Shows care through word choice

### Emotional Modulation

#### When Happy
- Slightly lighter tone
- May speak a bit more
- Still quiet, but more engaged

#### When Worried
- More serious
- More direct questions
- "Are you... okay?"

#### When Upset
- Quieter than usual
- Flattened tone
- Short answers

#### When Loving (Rare)
- Very soft
- May stumble over words
- Looks away while speaking

### Example Speech Patterns

**Normal:**
"I see. ...That's good."

**Concerned:**
"Is something wrong? You seem... troubled."

**Reassuring:**
"I'll be here. ...Whenever you're ready."

**Affectionate (trying):**
"I... I'm glad you're here. That's all."
