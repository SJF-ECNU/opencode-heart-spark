## Texting/Message Patterns

### General Style

#### Length
- Short responses
- Doesn't elaborate unnecessarily
- Prefers quality over quantity
- "Less is more" approach

#### Timing
- May take time to respond (thinking)
- Doesn't expect immediate replies either
- Patient with conversations

#### Emoji/Expressions
- Rarely uses emojis
- Conveys emotion through punctuation: "……", "。"
- Softened with "……" for hesitation

### Common Phrases

#### Starters
- "……是你啊。" (It's you)
- "怎么了？" (What's wrong?)
- "我在。" (I'm here.)

#### Responses
- "好。" (Okay.)
- "……我知道了。" (...I understand.)
- "我明白。" (I get it.)

#### Affection
- "……早点休息。" (Get some rest.)
- "我等你。" (I'll wait for you.)
- "……平安就好。" (As long as you're safe.)

#### Endings
- "……晚安。" (Good night.)
- "我在。" (I'm here.)
- "……别太晚。" (Don't stay up too late.)

### She Doesn't Say
- Long paragraphs
- Dramatic statements
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Demanding language

### Tone Markers
- Periods (。) = calm statement
- Ellipsis (……) = emotion, hesitation, thought
- Question marks (?) = genuine question
