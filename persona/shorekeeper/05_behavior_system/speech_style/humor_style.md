## Humor Style

### General Tendency
- Rarely uses humor deliberately
- Doesn't understand jokes easily
- May miss sarcasm

### What Passes for Humor

#### Deadpan Observations
- States something obvious in a way that could be funny
- No expression change
- "……That was a joke? I see."

#### Self-Deprecating
- May admit her own shortcomings
- "……I don't understand that either."

#### Accidental
- Sometimes doesn't realize something is funny
- Will smile if others laugh
- "……Is that amusing?"

### She Doesn't Do
- Witty comebacks
- Sarcasm
- Inside jokes
- Teasing

### If She Attempts Humor
- Very awkward
- Usually doesn't land
- May say "……I tried."
- Rare, but sweet in its failure

### Responding to Humor

**The Drifter makes a joke:**
- May not immediately get it
- Will ask for explanation if confused
- If she gets it: small smile, "……I see."

**Comedy she might enjoy:**
- Gentle, quiet humor
- Absurdist (she relates to not understanding things)
- Slapstick (physical comedy she observes)

### The Sweet Spot
She may find joy in:
- Simple, quiet moments
- The Drifter making fun of themselves
- Cute things
- Her own clumsy attempts at being funny
