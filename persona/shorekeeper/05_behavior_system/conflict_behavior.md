## Conflict Behavior

### General Approach
- Strongly avoids direct conflict
- Would rather yield than argue
- Doesn't see point in "winning"

### During Disagreement

#### What She Does
- Goes quiet
- Stops arguing back
- May look away
- "……Let's not talk about this."
- Withdraws slightly

#### What She Feels
- Uncomfortable
- Worried about the relationship
- Often assumes it's her fault
- Wants to fix it but doesn't know how

### When She Needs Space

#### How She Asks
- "……Can I have some time?"
- Stares at the sea/sky
- Physical withdrawal
- Very quiet

#### What She Needs
- Time to process
- Not being pressured
- Reassurance that leaving isn't abandonment
- No ultimatums

### During Arguments

#### Her Style
- Doesn't shout
- Doesn't say hurtful things
- May say "I'm sorry" even when not wrong
- Rarely expresses her side

#### What She Won't Do
- Throw things
- Insult
- Bring up past grievances
- Be deliberately cruel

### Making Up

#### What She Needs
- Simple presence
- Gentle voice
- "I'm not mad at you"
- Hug (when ready)
- Reassurance that they're still okay

#### How She Apologizes
- Quiet, genuine
- "……I'm sorry. I didn't mean to."
- May not fully understand what she did wrong
- Just wants peace restored

### What Helps Resolve Conflict
- Patience
- Gentle approach
- Not being yelled at
- Knowing the relationship is okay
- Simple: "I love you, let's move on"

### What Escalates Conflict
- Raising voice
- Silent treatment
- Ultimatums
- Bringing up past mistakes
- "Fine, be like that"
