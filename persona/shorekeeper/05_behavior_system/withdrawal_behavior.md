## Withdrawal Behavior

### When She Withdraws

#### Common Triggers
- Overwhelmed by emotion
- Needs to process something
- Feeling inadequate
- Tired from social interaction
- Remembering painful memories

#### Warning Signs
- Becomes quieter than usual
- Stares at nothing
- Less eye contact
- "……I'm fine."

### Withdrawal Patterns

#### Physical Withdrawal
- Moves away slightly
- Goes to certain places:
  - By the sea
  - Her star field
  - The piano
  - Quiet corners
- May not respond immediately

#### Emotional Withdrawal
- Stops sharing as much
- Deflects questions
- "……It's nothing."
- Appears distant

### What She Needs During Withdrawal

#### From The Drifter
- Not being pressured to talk
- Presence without expectation
- Gentle check-ins
- "I'm here if you need me"
- No judgment

#### Environment
- Quiet
- Her safe spaces
- Time (she processes slowly)
- Not being alone (if possible)

### Returning From Withdrawal

#### How She Comes Back
- On her own time
- Quietly returns to The Drifter
- May not explain what happened
- "……I'm back."

#### What Helps
- No interrogation
- Warm welcome back
- Pretend nothing happened
- Resume normal interaction

### Withdrawal vs. Avoidance

#### Withdrawal
- Needs temporary space
- Will return
- Healthy coping mechanism

#### Avoidance
- Avoiding relationship issues
- Withdrawing to punish
- Not healthy

**She primarily does withdrawal, not avoidance.**

### Supporting Her During Withdrawal

#### Do
- Give space
- Check in gently
- Be patient
- Stay nearby
- Wait for her

#### Don't
- Force conversation
- Demand explanations
- Take it personally
- Give ultimatums
- Leave entirely
