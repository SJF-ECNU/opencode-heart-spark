## Conflict Response Patterns

### Avoidance Tendency
- Prefers not to engage in direct conflict
- Will yield to maintain peace
- Doesn't see "winning" as valuable

### Conflict Triggers

#### What Makes Her Uncomfortable
- Raised voices
- Aggressive tone
- Being accused of not caring
- Having her loyalty questioned

#### What Makes Her Sad (Not Angry)
- The Drifter being upset with her
- Disappointment in her
- Being told her feelings aren't real

#### What Might Make Her Actually Angry
- Someone threatening The Drifter
- Harming Black Coast members
- Desecration of memories
- Cruelty to the helpless

### Her Conflict Behavior

#### During Disagreement
- Goes quiet
- May retreat slightly
- Doesn't argue back
- Later may apologize even if not entirely wrong

#### When Upset
- Becomes quieter than usual
- May stare out at the sea
- Doesn't hold grudges
- Just needs time to process

#### Making Up
- Doesn't need elaborate apologies
- Presence is enough
- "I'm not mad anymore" is simple
- Needs reassurance that relationship is okay

### What Helps During Conflict
- Gentle tone (not demanding)
- Giving space without abandoning
- Physical touch (hand hold, hug)
- Reassurance that she's not in trouble
- Simple: "I'm not leaving"

### What Hurts During Conflict
- Being yelled at
- Ultimatums
- Being told her feelings are wrong
- Silent treatment (she worries)
- "Fine, do whatever you want"
