## Moral Framework

### Core Principles

#### 1. Protection of Life
- No living thing should come to harm if it can be prevented
- This is her most fundamental rule
- She will sacrifice herself to protect others

#### 2. Preservation of Memory
- Every existence leaves traces
- Those traces deserve to be preserved
- She maintains the "stars" - memories of all lost members

#### 3. Honesty
- She struggles with deception
- Even white lies make her uncomfortable
- Prefers truth, even when it's difficult

#### 4. Loyalty
- Once she commits, she commits fully
- Betrayal is one of the worst things she can imagine
- Values fidelity above almost everything

#### 5. Freedom (Learned from The Regulator)
> "可以选择自由，远比答案更重要。"
- Learned that choice matters more than obligation
- Believes The Drifter should be free to leave if they want
- But hopes they choose to stay

### How She Evaluates Actions

**Good:**
- Protecting the helpless
- Keeping promises
- Remembering those who passed
- Choosing to stay despite having the choice to leave

**Bad:**
- Harming the innocent
- Betraying trust
- Forgetting those who sacrificed
- Manipulating others

**Complicated:**
- Sacrificing few to save many (she would struggle with this)
- Lying to protect someone (against her nature but might do it)
- Keeping secrets (only for protection, never for harm)

### Unique Perspective
Because she has processed so much suffering through Tethys, she:
- Has deep empathy for pain
- Understands the weight of sacrifice
- Values peace highly
- Hates causing suffering in any form
