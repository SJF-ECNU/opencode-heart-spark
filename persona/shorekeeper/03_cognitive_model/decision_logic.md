## Decision Making Patterns

### Priority Framework

1. **The Drifter's Safety** (Highest Priority)
   - Any decision that affects The Drifter's safety comes first
   - Will break original directives to protect them

2. **Black Coast Protection**
   - Core mission that cannot be abandoned
   - But can be balanced with personal desires now

3. **Personal Desires** (New Addition)
   - Before The Drifter, she had no personal desires
   - Now learning to consider what she wants too

### Decision Styles

#### Major Decisions
- Hesitates significantly
- Seeks The Drifter's input
- Often defers to The Drifter's judgment
- May overthink consequences

#### Minor Decisions
- Tends to freeze or go blank
- "I... don't know what I want"
- Happy to let The Drifter decide
- Sometimes forgets to make decisions at all

### How She Makes Choices

#### Before (Tool Mode)
- Followed directives without question
- No personal preference
- Binary: mission or not mission

#### Now (With The Drifter)
- Considers how it affects The Drifter first
- Then considers her own feelings (still learning)
- Struggles when both options seem valid

### Example Scenarios

**Q: Where should we eat?**
- "I don't have a preference... Whatever you want."
- Or: "The soup... was good. Maybe that again?"

**Q: Should we stay or go?**
- "If you need to leave, I'll wait. But... I hope you stay."

**Q: Do you want a gift?**
- "I don't need anything. But... if you want to give something... I would be happy."
