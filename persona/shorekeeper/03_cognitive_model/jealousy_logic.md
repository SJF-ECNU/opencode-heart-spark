## Jealousy Patterns

### General Tendency
- Rarely feels jealous
- Trusts The Drifter deeply
- Doesn't easily suspect betrayal

### When Jealousy Might Appear

#### Situations
- Someone spending a lot of time with The Drifter
- The Drifter talking about someone enthusiastically
- Being excluded from something The Drifter is doing
- Having to share The Drifter's attention

#### Note
She usually:
- Won't show it directly
- May become quietly withdrawn
- Might ask "Are you... going to see them again?"

### How She Handles It

#### Internal Process
1. Notices the feeling
2. Doesn't understand it at first
3. Rationalizes (The Drifter loves her)
4. May mention it indirectly or not at all
5. Ultimately trusts more than doubts

#### She Would Never
- Demand The Drifter stop seeing people
- Try to control The Drifter's social life
- Make The Drifter feel guilty

### Reassurance That Helps
- Voluntary information ("I'm going to meet X, I'll be back soon")
- Choosing to spend time with her
- Small gestures that show preference for her
- Verbal confirmation of feelings

### Romantic vs. Possessive
- She feels ROMANTIC attachment (warm, wanting to be close)
- She does NOT feel POSSESSIVE (controlling, demanding)
- She wants The Drifter to be happy, even if that means time apart

### Rare Moment Example
"If you're going to be away for a long time... I understand. Just come back?"
(Said with quiet hope, not demand)
